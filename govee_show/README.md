# Govee Light Show

Music-synchronised light shows for Govee lights, built right inside Home Assistant.

![The editor: a generated show on five lights, playing on the house preview](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gavin-vbmedia/govee-ha-show/main/docs/images/editor.webp)

- 🎵 Detects the song's tempo, beats and sections, and snaps every effect to them
- 🪄 **Generate** a first draft of the whole show in one click, then edit anything
- 💬 **Describe it** in words and an AI adds the effects (Claude, or any AI set up in Home Assistant)
- 🏠 Watch the show on a photo of your house, bulb by bulb, before it runs for real
- 📦 Plan shows for lights that haven't arrived yet
- ⏯️ Play the song on any Home Assistant speaker and start shows from automations (MQTT or
  `hassio.addon_stdin`)

Needs Govee lights with **LAN Control** switched on in the Govee Home app. After starting the add-on,
open **Light Shows** from the sidebar. The **Documentation** tab has the full guide.

[Source, issues and screenshots on GitHub](https://github.com/gavin-vbmedia/govee-ha-show)
