Add a speaker

Press Test and add next to a speaker. It plays a short chime the same way a show starts, and the speaker is added only if that works.

Why can't some speakers play shows?

Only speakers added here are offered in the editor. Adding one plays a short chime on it the same way a show starts, so a speaker that can't play the add-on's songs is caught here rather than when the show should start. Some media players, like Echo devices through Alexa Media Player or Emby clients, can't play files from Home Assistant's media folder.

Automations

To play this show from an automation, use the HA-liday Show MQTT entities (pick the show, then press Play), or the hassio.addon_stdin action with input {"action": "play", "show": "…"}. The song plays on the show's Show speaker (in the editor's ⚙ Settings… for the show), or on the Default show speaker above when a show doesn't pick one. The speaker in the Playback panel is only for editing, so you can design on a speaker indoors without changing where the real show plays.

How to trace your lights

Take the photo from where people will watch, ideally at dusk. Then pick a light in the list, press Trace and click along where it runs on the photo, starting at the end with the plug. Click once at each corner or bend. Drag a point to move it, right-click a point to remove it, and press Enter or Done when the light is traced. Each light is shown in a rainbow from the plug (red) to the far end, so you can check its direction. The editor's House panel then plays the show on the photo.

Part of a light out of sight, like round the back of the house, or an extension lead? Trace past it anyway (a short line will do), then click that stretch and choose Out of view with how many bulbs are on it. Those bulbs still take their share of the segments but aren't drawn.

Layout

Pick a page layout to set how the five areas fit together, then choose where each editor panel sits: along the top or bottom, down the left or right, or in the middle. Choose Hidden to tuck a panel away and bring it back here. In the editor you can also drag a panel by its title to move it or change its order, or use the ⋯ button on it. The layout is saved in this browser.

AI

Checking…

The Describe it panel turns a request in words into effects, using either Claude with your own API key (set it on the add-on's Configuration tab in Home Assistant), or else Home Assistant's AI Task, which uses whichever AI integration you have set up there, including a local one such as Ollama. It sends the AI your lights' names, the effect list, the song's beats and sections, and the selection; never the song itself.

Experimental

Adds a Govee scene effect that hands a light to one of the scenes built into the Govee app (the Scenes list, not your saved snapshots) for the length of the clip. It uses an unofficial command and Govee's public scene list, so it may not work on every light. The editor's preview can't show the scene itself, and the light may blink as it switches in and out. Saved in this browser.

Packet lab

For trying out commands the add-on doesn't use yet. Pick one of your lights and a starting point, edit the message if you like, and press Send. Anything the light sends back is listed below. A playing show is stopped first. Only lights on the Devices tab can be sent to.

Reaching past the realtime window

Some lights, like the 30 m Christmas String Lights 2, only take realtime colours for their first few dozen bulbs. Run sweep tries nine guesses at how the rest might be reached, one after another, each in its own colour, using the size of the window (44 on the H70C5, or Segments above for a light whose window isn't known). Film the whole light while it runs: a colour showing up past the window means that guess works. It takes about 45 seconds.

A TV sync box does get colour onto the whole of these lights, so it may switch them into a different mode first. Try ways to switch on tries seven guesses at that, one after another, each followed by a rainbow over the window. Watch for the rainbow spreading past the window or along the whole light, and note the number shown below when it does. It takes about 50 seconds.

Try other flags sends a whole frame of the window in one colour, nine times, each with a different value in the byte that switches crisp or blended colours (only 00, 01 and 02 have been tried). A colour lighting more than the first bulbs means that value reaches further. It takes about 45 seconds.

Try app modes first switches the light into each of twenty Govee app modes in turn, then sends the usual realtime frame of the window in that step's colour. Between steps the light goes plain white. Watch for a colour reaching past the window, or anything other than the first bulbs lighting up. It takes about a minute and a half.

Per bulb speed test

For lights on Per bulb (experimental): changes the picture for six seconds, using Segments above as the number of bulbs: the first 10 bulbs flip between red and blue, or a red, green and blue pattern moves along every bulb. Watch whether the light keeps up or skips some. Try both, at a few rates.
